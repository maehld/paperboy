Device

The plan assigns the pages; without one the device shows a notice page. Plans are the files under plans/ and are edited on their own page.

Refresh and menu

The menu opens on a short home press; deny turns it off (ADR 0057). The duration is between 5s and 1m. touch controls draws the close box and the page arrows under the actions for a digitiser; without it the menu is button-only (ADR 0080).

An empty field keeps the display type's setting or the default.

Battery warning

Empty values inherit defaults.battery of paperboy.yaml (ADR 0020).

Links

The preview shows the plan the device uses, page by page (ADR 0054).
